## Intervention Protocol

### Elastic Band Resistance Training (EBT)

> This is the **pilot study** for the subsequent larger RCT (NCT06800079). It establishes feasibility, protocol adherence, and preliminary effect sizes.

**Programme Overview:**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Duration | 6 weeks |
| Frequency | 2 sessions/week |
| Session timing | Before regular training session (pre-activation) |
| Session length | ~20 minutes |
| Setting | In-season |

**Exercise Structure:**
- 6 exercises per programme (split across 2 sessions)
- Exercises target lower extremity strength, stability, and power

**Assessments:**
- Week 1 (baseline)
- Week 6 (post-intervention)

---

## Outcome Measures

### Primary Outcomes

| Measure | Tool | Protocol | Timeframe |
|---------|------|----------|-----------|
| Knee extension isometric strength | DynaMo Plus handheld dynamometer (VALD Performance) | Seated; knee at ~60°; maximal isometric effort × 5 sec | Week 1 → Week 6 |
| Knee flexion isometric strength | DynaMo Plus handheld dynamometer | Prone; knee at ~30°; maximal isometric effort × 5 sec | Week 1 → Week 6 |
| Vertical jump (CMJ) | OptoJump optical system (Microgate) | Upright start → rapid descent to ~90° knee flexion → maximal jump | Week 1 → Week 6 |
| Change of direction sprint | Modified T-Test (photocells: WhittyGate, Microgate) | Forward sprint + lateral shuffle (left & right) + backpedal | Week 1 → Week 6 |

## Relationship to NCT06800079

This pilot study directly informed the design of the larger multi-level RCT (NCT06800079), which:
- Extended duration to 8 weeks
- Added randomization (EG vs. CG)
- Expanded to U15, U19, and Senior cohorts (n=60)
- Upgraded jump assessment to ForceDecks force plates

---

## Practical Takeaways for Coaches

1. **Pre-activation EBT** (20 min before training) is a time-efficient in-season strategy
2. **OptoJump or similar optical systems** are accessible alternatives to force plates for CMJ assessment
3. **Modified T-Test** is a validated, low-cost CoD assessment requiring minimal space
4. **Handheld dynamometry** (DynaMo Plus) provides reliable isometric strength data without isokinetic equipment
