## Intervention Protocol

### Experimental Group — Strength Training Programme

> Full protocol details were not publicly disclosed in the trial registration, but the intervention is described as a **structured strength training programme** targeting lower-limb performance and inter-limb asymmetry correction.

**Duration:** 12 weeks  
**Frequency:** Not specified (typical for S&C in this context: 2–3×/week)

**Training Focus Areas (inferred from outcome measures):**
1. Vertical jump (countermovement jump, drop jump, standing broad jump)
2. Sprint speed (40m with 10m/20m/30m splits)
3. Change of direction (180° CoD, V-cut test)
4. Inter-limb strength symmetry

**Asymmetry Correction Formula Used:**
```
Inter-limb asymmetry (%) = [100 / Max(R,L)] × Min(R,L) × -1 + 100
```

### Control Group
- Normal football training only — no additional strength programme

## Contextual Background (from trial description)

Elite female soccer players cover:
- ~10 km total distance per match
- ~1.7 km at high speed (>18 km/h)
- 1,350–1,650 activity changes per match
- 5.1–31.2 repeated sprint/high-intensity bouts

This highlights the **critical importance of speed, power, and CoD ability** for female footballers, and the need for sport-specific S&C interventions.

---

## Key Research Questions Addressed

1. Can a structured strength training programme improve sprint speed in female soccer players aged 12–20?
2. Can it improve vertical and horizontal jump height?
3. Can it reduce inter-limb strength/power asymmetry — a known injury risk factor?
4. How does it affect change-of-direction ability?

---

## Practical Takeaways for Coaches

1. **Inter-limb asymmetry testing should be standard** in female football — it is both an injury risk marker and a performance variable
2. **A 12-week block** is a more realistic minimum for meaningful performance transfer
3. **Unilateral exercises** should be included specifically to address limb dominance imbalances
4. **V-cut and 180° CoD tests** are recommended assessment tools for this population
