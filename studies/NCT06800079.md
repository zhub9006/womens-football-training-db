## Intervention Protocol

### Experimental Group — Elastic Band Training (EBT)

**Programme Overview:**

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Duration | 8 weeks |
| Frequency | 2 sessions/week |
| Session length | ~20 minutes |
| Setting | In-season (incorporated before regular training) |
| Total sessions | ~16 |
| Exercises per programme | 6 (split across 2 sessions) |

**Familiarisation Week (Week 0):**
- Demonstration of all assessments and training exercises
- Anthropometric measurements (height, weight)
- Baseline assessments conducted
- Questionnaire: training habits + injury history

**Randomisation:**
- Experimental Group (EG): performs EBT protocol
- Control Group (CG): continues standard training only

**Assessments:** Week 0 (pre) → Week 9 (post)

## Outcome Measures

### Primary Outcomes

| Measure | Tool | Protocol | Timeframe |
|---------|------|----------|-----------|
| Knee extension isometric strength | DynaMo Plus handheld dynamometer (VALD Performance) | Seated; knee at ~60°; maximal isometric effort × 5 sec; no compensatory movements | Week 0 → Week 9 |
| Knee flexion isometric strength | DynaMo Plus handheld dynamometer | Prone; knee at ~30°; maximal isometric effort × 5 sec | Week 0 → Week 9 |
| Countermovement jump (CMJ) height | ForceDecks force plates (VALD Performance) | Upright start → rapid descent to ~90° knee flexion → maximal vertical jump | Week 0 → Week 9 |
| Change of direction sprint | Modified T-Test (WhittyGate photocells, Microgate) | Forward sprint + left/right lateral shuffle + backpedal | Week 0 → Week 9 |

---

## Key Innovation: Multi-Level Comparison

This is the **first study to directly compare EBT effects across three female football development phases simultaneously:**

| Age Group | Development Phase | Expected Adaptation Differences |
|-----------|------------------|----------------------------------|
| U15 | Early specialisation | Larger relative strength gains (lower baseline) |
| U19 | Late specialisation | Intermediate responses |
| Senior | Performance phase | Smaller relative gains (higher baseline) but greater functional transfer |

## Practical Takeaways for Coaches

1. **Elastic bands are accessible** — this protocol requires only elastic bands and can be done anywhere
2. **8 weeks at 2×/week** is the minimum effective dose being tested for EBT in-season
3. **Development phase matters** — U15 players may respond differently than Senior players; programming should be age-differentiated
4. **ForceDecks force plates** provide the most comprehensive jump analysis (force-time curve, reactive strength index, etc.)
5. **FPF collaboration** suggests this protocol may be adopted at national federation level
